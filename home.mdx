## Get Watching in Seconds

Z-Stream requires zero setup. There's no account to create, no payment information to enter, and no app to download.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to zstream.mov">
    Open [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov) in any modern web browser on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find something to watch">
    Browse by genre, check out the Most Popular or Top Rated lists, or explore Editor Picks for curated recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Play">
    Click on any title and hit the play button.
  </Step>
</Steps>
