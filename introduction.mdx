## Welcome to Z-Stream

Z-Stream is a completely free streaming platform where you can watch thousands of movies and TV shows right in your browser. All you have to do is go to [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov), search for or find a movie, and click play. No setup or subscription needed.

<Note>
  **Disclaimer:** Z-Stream does not host or store any media files on its servers. All content is provided by third-party sources, and we simply index and link to publicly available media hosted elsewhere.
</Note>

## What You Can Watch

Z-Stream offers a broad and regularly updated library of content across multiple categories:

### Movies

Browse thousands of films spanning every genre — action, comedy, drama, horror, sci-fi, romance, and more. Use genre filters to narrow down your options, or explore curated lists to find something new.

### TV Shows

Stream full TV series with complete season and episode navigation. Whether you're starting a new show from the beginning or picking up where you left off, Z-Stream makes it easy to find and queue up any episode.

### For You

Not sure what to watch? The **For You** section highlights titles randomly generated based on your watch history, bookmarks and ratings. If you don't like it, you can change it by taking a simple 4-step quiz.

### Genre Browsing

Z-Stream organizes its entire catalog by genre, so you can dive straight into the type of content you're in the mood for. Filter movies and shows independently to keep results relevant.

### Popular & Top-Rated Lists

See what other viewers are watching right now with the **Popular** list, or explore critically acclaimed titles through the **Top Rated** section. Both lists update regularly to reflect current trends and timeless favorites.

## Live TV with Z-Live

Looking for live sports, news, or broadcast channels? Z-Stream's affiliated live TV service, **Z-Live**, is available at [zlive.st](https://zlive.st). It offers a wide range of live channels — sports events, news feeds, entertainment — all free and in the same no-account spirit as Z-Stream.
