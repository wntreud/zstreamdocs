## Welcome to Z-Stream

Z-Stream is a completely free streaming platform where you can watch thousands of movies and TV shows right in your browser. All you have to do is go to [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov), search for or find a movie, and click play. No setup or subscription needed.

### For You

Not sure what to watch? The **For You** section highlights titles generated based on your watch history, bookmarks, and ratings. If you don't like it, you can change it by taking a simple quiz at [zstream.mov/algorithm](https://zstream.mov/algorithm).

## Live TV with Z-Live

Looking for live sports, news, or broadcast channels? Z-Stream's affiliated live TV service, **Z-Live**, is available at [zlive.st](https://zlive.st). It offers a wide range of live channels — sports events, news feeds, entertainment — all free and in the same no-account spirit as Z-Stream.
