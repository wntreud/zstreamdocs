Z-Stream is a free, community-powered platform. If you run into a problem, have a question, or want to request a title, the Discord server is where you'll find the fastest help — from both the team and fellow users.

<Note>
  Z-Stream is a free, community-supported platform. There is no paid support tier or dedicated helpdesk — the Discord community is the primary and fastest way to get assistance. Response times may vary depending on community activity.
</Note>

Saving yourself a wait is easy — many common issues are already covered in the help docs. Check these first:

- [**Troubleshooting Playback Issues**](/help/troubleshooting) — blank screens, error messages, and browser compatibility problems.
- [**Buffering & Quality**](/help/buffering-and-quality) — slow streams, stuttering, and quality settings.
- [**Missing Content**](/help/missing-content) — why a title might not be available and how to request it.
- [**General FAQ**](/faq/general) — answers to the most frequently asked questions about Z-Stream.

If you've already checked those and still need help, the Discord community is the right place to go.

## What You Can Do on Discord

Once you're in the server, here's how the community can help you:

- **Ask for help** — describe the issue you're experiencing (include your browser, OS, and the title you're trying to watch) and community members will point you in the right direction.
- **Report issues** — if you've found a bug, a broken player, or a source that's gone dead, report it so it can be fixed.
- **Request content** — post in the requests channel to ask for a movie or TV show to be added to the catalog.
- **Give feedback** — share suggestions for improving the platform. The team actively reads community feedback.
- **Chat with other users** — connect with other Z-Stream fans, get recommendations, and discuss what you're watching.

## Tips for Getting Help Quickly

Following these steps helps the community assist you faster:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search before posting">
    Use Discord's search function to check if your question has already been answered in a recent thread. Many common issues come up frequently and already have solutions posted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide clear details">
    When describing a problem, include:

    - The title of the movie or show (and season/episode if applicable)
    - What happened (error message, blank screen, buffering, etc.)
    - Your browser name and version
    - Your operating system (Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, etc.)
    - What you've already tried

    More detail means a faster, more accurate answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Be patient">
    Z-Stream is a free platform run by a community. Responses aren't instant — give it some time, especially if you post outside of peak community hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## About Z-Stream

Z-Stream ([zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov)) is a free, no-account movie and TV streaming platform — the successor to P-Stream (formerly pstream.net). There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install. Just visit the site and start watching.
