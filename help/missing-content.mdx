Z-Stream's catalog is large and grows constantly, but not every title is available at all times. If you can't find what you're looking for, this page explains the most likely reasons and what you can do to get the title added or track it down.

<Note>
  Z-Stream's catalog is continuously updated. If a title isn't available right now, it may be indexed soon — check back in a few days, or jump into the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) to request it directly.
</Note>

## Why a Title Might Not Be Available

There are a few reasons a movie or TV show may not appear on Z-Stream:

- **Not yet indexed** — the title exists but hasn't been added to Z-Stream's catalog yet. New releases and older titles are added regularly as sources become available.
- **Temporarily unavailable** — a title that was previously available may have had its source link go offline. This is usually temporary while the source is re-indexed or replaced.
- **Very new release** — titles that have just left theaters or been released digitally may not have been sourced yet. Check back after a few days.
- **Rare or obscure content** — very niche titles, older foreign-language films, or limited-release content may simply not have an available source yet.

<Note>
  Z-Stream does not apply regional restrictions — the platform is free and open to everyone, globally, with no account required.
</Note>

## How to Search Effectively

Before concluding that a title is missing, try these search techniques:

- **Use the exact title** — search using the full, official title of the movie or show, exactly as it appears on other platforms or on its poster.
- **Try alternate spellings or transliterations** — some titles have multiple spellings (especially foreign-language titles or remakes). Try a few variations.
- **Search by year** — if a title is common (for example, a remake with the same name as the original), add the release year to narrow the results.
- **Check for series vs. seasons** — TV shows are usually listed under the main series title. Search for the show name first, then navigate to the season you want from the title page.
- **Use partial titles** — if the full title returns no results, try searching for a key word from the title rather than the full phrase.

## 'Not in Catalog' vs. 'Temporarily Unavailable'

Understanding the difference helps you decide what to do next:

| Situation | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No search results found** | The title is not currently in Z-Stream's catalog | Request it on Discord or check back later |
| **Title appears but player shows no source** | The title is indexed but its source is currently broken or offline | Try a different source in the player, or check back in a day or two |
| **Title was available before but is now gone** | The source link went dead or was removed | Report it in Discord so it can be re-sourced |

## How to Request Content

If a title you want isn't in the catalog, the best way to request it is through the Z-Stream community on Discord.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord server">
    Go to [discord.gg/streaming](https://discord.gg/streaming) and join the Z-Stream community server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the content request channel">
    Look for a channel dedicated to **#support** in the channel list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post your request">
    Share the full title, release year, and (if applicable) the season and episode you're looking for. The more specific you are, the easier it is to fulfill your request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check back on Z-Stream">
    Once a request is fulfilled, the title will appear in the catalog. You'll usually be notified in the Discord thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Do If a Previously Available Title Disappeared

If you watched something on Z-Stream before and it's no longer there, the most likely cause is that the source link became inactive. Here's what to do:

1. **Search again** — occasionally titles are re-indexed under a slightly different name.
2. **Try directly browsing the catalog** — navigate through the genre or Discover pages to see if the title appears there even if the search doesn't surface it.
3. **Report it in Discord** — let the community know in the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/streaming). Mention the title, year, and roughly when you last watched it. The team or community members can often re-source it quickly.

<Tip>
  If your specific title isn't available, try the **Discover** page on Z-Stream to browse similar content. You might find something just as good while you wait for your title to be added.
</Tip>
