## Why Z-Stream Runs Ads

Z-Stream is completely free, with no subscription, no signup, and no paywalls. Ads are how the platform covers the cost of servers, bandwidth, and continued development. Running ads is what keeps the site online and free for everyone.

<Note>
  Z-Stream will never charge you, ask for payment details, or lock content behind a paid tier. Ads are the only way the service is funded.
</Note>

## What to Expect

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Display Ads" icon="rectangle-ad">
    Banner and display ads may appear around the site as you browse.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Player Ads" icon="circle-play">
    Some streams may include ads served by the source or player itself. These are not controlled directly by Z-Stream.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No Account Data Sold" icon="user-shield">
    Z-Stream does not require an account and does not sell personal data. See the [Privacy FAQ](/faq/privacy) for details.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Occasional Pop-ups" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Third-party ad networks occasionally serve pop-ups or redirects. Close them and return to the site. Never install anything an ad prompts you to install.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Staying Safe Around Ads

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Never install software from an ad">
    Z-Stream will never ask you to install a browser extension, "video codec," or "update" through an ad or pop-up. If a page tells you to install something to keep watching, close it. Only download official apps from [zstream.mov/apps](https://zstream.mov/apps).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verify you're on the official domain">
    The only official domain is **zstream.mov**. Copycat sites may impersonate Z-Stream and serve unsafe ads. Always check the URL, and see the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) for confirmed mirrors.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Report bad ads">
    If you see a malicious ad, a scam pop-up, or an ad that breaks playback, report it in the [Z-Stream Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) with a screenshot and the URL. The team can flag and block problem networks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ad Blockers Are Welcome

Z-Stream fully supports the use of ad blockers such as **uBlock Origin**. Blocking ads will not lock you out of the site or its content.

<Warning>
  Some ad blockers or aggressive privacy extensions can accidentally block the video sources or scrapers Z-Stream uses to play content. If a video fails to load or you see a **"Failed to scrape"** error, try disabling your ad blocker or VPN on the site as a first troubleshooting step. See the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) for more.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If you'd like to support Z-Stream without the trade-offs of ad blocking, the best thing you can do is share the site with friends and stay active in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/streaming).
</Tip>

## Ads in the Native Apps

The native [Z-Stream apps](/apps) for Android, Android TV, iOS, and Windows may also display ads to support development. As with the website, no purchase or subscription is ever required to use the apps.
