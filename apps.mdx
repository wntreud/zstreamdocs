## Native Apps

Z-Stream works entirely in the browser, but native apps are available for a faster and more seamless streaming experience. Apps offer better performance, native playback controls, and full-screen support out of the box.

<Note>
  All Z-Stream apps are completely free. No account, subscription, or payment is ever required.
</Note>

## Installation Notes

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Android">
    Android installs are provided as an APK. You may need to enable **Install from Unknown Sources** in your device settings before installing. Always download the APK from the official [zstream.mov/apps](https://zstream.mov/apps) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV">
    Sideload the TV APK using a file manager or a tool like Downloader. Once installed, Z-Stream will appear in your app drawer and can be launched with your remote.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="iOS">
    Follow the installation steps listed on the official apps page. iOS installs may require trusting the developer profile in **Settings \> General \> VPN & Device Management** after installing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Windows">
    Download the installer from [zstream.mov/apps](https://zstream.mov/apps) and run it. Windows 10 or 11 is recommended.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Prefer the Web?

You don't need an app to use Z-Stream. Everything works in your browser at [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov). See [Supported Devices](/supported-devices) for a full list of compatible browsers and platforms.
