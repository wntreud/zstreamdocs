## Native Apps

Native apps are available for a faster and more seamless streaming experience. Apps offer better performance, native playback controls, and better sources. @ [https://zstream.mov/apps](https://zstream.mov/apps)

<Note>
  All Z-Stream apps are completely free with no ads. No payment is ever required.
</Note>

## Why Use the App?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Better Performance/Sources" icon="gauge-high">
    Native apps deliver smoother playback and faster load times compared to the browser, especially on lower-powered devices.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Downloads" icon="download">
    Use native apps for downloading media to watch offline. This is the recommended way to download from Z-Stream.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Optimized for TV" icon="tv">
    The Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV build is designed for remote-based navigation, giving you a true 10-foot experience.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sync Across Devices" icon="cloud-arrow-up">
    Sign in with your username and password to keep your settings and watch history synced between the app and the web.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
