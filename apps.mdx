## Native Apps

Z-Stream works entirely in the browser, but native apps are available for a faster and more seamless streaming experience. Apps offer better performance, native playback controls, and full-screen support out of the box.

<Note>
  All Z-Stream apps are completely free. No account, subscription, or payment is ever required.
</Note>

## Installation Guides

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Android">
    Android installs are provided as an APK. You may need to enable **Install from Unknown Sources** in your device settings before installing. Always download the APK from the official [zstream.mov/apps](https://zstream.mov/apps) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV">
    Sideload the TV APK using a file manager or a tool like Downloader. Once installed, Z-Stream will appear in your app drawer and can be launched with your remote.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="iOS">
    **Installing ZStream on iPhone & iPad**

    ZStream is installed by sideloading an IPA file. We recommend **SideStore** because it's free, easy to use, and only requires a computer for the initial setup.

    #### Recommended Method: SideStore

    ### Requirements

    - iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 15 or later
    - Windows, macOS, Linux, or Chromebook (only needed for the initial setup)
    - Apple ID
    - USB cable
    - Internet connection

    ## Step 1: Install SideStore

    Before installing ZStream, you'll need to set up SideStore.

    Official installation guide:

    [https://docs.sidestore.io/docs/installation/prerequisites](https://docs.sidestore.io/docs/installation/prerequisites)

    If you prefer a video guide, here are some helpful tutorials:

    - Windows: [https://www.youtube.com/results?search\_query=SideStore\+Windows\+Installation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SideStore+Windows+Installation)
    - macOS: [https://www.youtube.com/results?search\_query=SideStore\+Mac\+Installation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SideStore+Mac+Installation)
    - Linux: [https://www.youtube.com/results?search\_query=SideStore\+Linux\+Installation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SideStore+Linux+Installation)

    Follow the SideStore setup guide until the SideStore app is installed on your iPhone or iPad.

    ## Step 2: Download ZStream

    Download the latest ZStream IPA from the Downloads page.

    ## Step 3: Install the IPA

    1. Open SideStore.
    2. Tap the **\+** button.
    3. Select the downloaded ZStream IPA.
    4. Wait for the installation to finish.
    5. Open ZStream from your Home Screen.

    ## Refreshing Apps

    Apps installed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days.

    To keep ZStream working:

    1. Open SideStore.
    2. Refresh your apps before they expire.

    SideStore makes refreshing simple and doesn't require reconnecting to a computer after the initial setup.

    ## Troubleshooting

    ### Installation Failed

    - Make sure SideStore is installed correctly.
    - Verify your internet connection.
    - Make sure the IPA downloaded completely.
    - Try installing again.

    ### Unable to Verify App

    - Ensure SideStore has been set up correctly.
    - Make sure the required SideStore VPN is enabled when refreshing or installing apps.

    ### App Expired

    Open SideStore and refresh the app before the 7-day signing period ends.<br /><br />Note: iOS installs may require trusting the developer profile in **Settings \> General \> VPN & Device Management** after installing.
  </Accordion>
