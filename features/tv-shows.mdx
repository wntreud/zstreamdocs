## Browsing by Genre

To filter TV shows by genre:

1. Go to [zstream.mov/discover](https://zstream.mov/discover).
2. Click the **TV Shows** tab at the top.
3. Select a genre from the genre selector.
4. The results grid updates immediately with matching series.

You can combine genre browsing with the Most Popular or Top Rated lists to surface the best-reviewed shows in any category.

## Navigating Seasons and Episodes

Click any show poster to open its detail page. From there you can:

- **Read the series description** to get a feel for the plot and tone
- **Select a season** from the season dropdown or tab list
- **Browse available episodes** within that season — each episode shows its title and number
- **Click an episode** to go straight to the player and start watching

Switching seasons is straightforward: choose the season you want from the selector and the episode list updates automatically. You can jump to any episode in any season without having to watch in order.
