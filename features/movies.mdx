## Movie Detail Pages

Click any movie poster to open its detail page. Here you'll find everything you need before you hit play:

- **Title and release year** — confirm you've found the right version
- **Genre tags** — quickly see how the film is categorised
- **Description** — a plot summary to help you decide if it's worth watching
- **Play button** — one click to start streaming immediately

No forms, no upsells, no waiting — just press play.

## How to Start Watching a Movie

1. Go to [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov) or the [Discover page](https://zstream.mov/discover).
2. Browse by genre, or pick from Most Popular, Top Rated, or In Cinemas.
3. Click a movie poster to open its detail page.
4. Press the **Play** button.
5. The movie starts streaming in your browser — no download or plugin required.

<Tip>
  Check the **In Cinemas** list for the latest theatrical releases — it's the quickest way to find movies that just hit the big screen and are freshest in the cultural conversation.
</Tip>
