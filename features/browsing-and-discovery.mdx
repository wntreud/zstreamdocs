Z-Stream gives you multiple ways to find something great to watch — no account, no algorithm lock-in, and no ads getting in the way. Whether you know exactly what you want or you're just browsing, the discovery tools at [zstream.mov/discover](https://zstream.mov/discover) make it easy.

## The Discover Section

Head to [zstream.mov/discover](https://zstream.mov/discover) for a full overview of what's available. From here you can jump into curated lists, filter by genre, or switch between the Movies and TV Shows catalogs — all from one place.

### Switching Between Movies and TV Shows

At the top of the Discover page, you'll find two tabs — **Movies** and **TV Shows**. Click either tab to scope every list and filter to that content type. Your genre selections and sort order carry over when you switch, so you can compare what's available across both catalogs without losing your place.

## Browsing by Genre

Z-Stream organises its entire catalog by genre so you can zero in on exactly the mood you're after. Available movie genres include:

- Action
- Adventure
- Animation
- Comedy
- Crime
- Drama
- Fantasy
- Horror
- Mystery
- Romance
- Sci-Fi
- Thriller
- Documentary
- Family
- History

To filter by genre, open the Discover page, select your content type (Movies or TV Shows), and choose a genre from the genre selector. The results grid updates instantly — no page reload required.

## Loading More Results

Every list on Z-Stream shows an initial set of results. When you reach the bottom of the grid, click the **Load More** button to pull in the next batch of titles. You can keep loading until you've seen everything in that category — useful for deep dives into a specific genre or list.

## Editor Picks

The Z-Stream team curates Editor Picks for both movies and TV shows separately. These selections go beyond popularity rankings and spotlight titles that are genuinely worth your time — overlooked classics, binge-worthy series, and seasonal watches. Check back regularly, as the picks are refreshed to keep the selection current.
