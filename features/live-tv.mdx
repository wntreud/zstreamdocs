Z-Stream's on-demand library covers movies and TV shows, but if you want to watch something live — a big match, a pay-per-view fight, or a live news channel — Z-Stream points you to its affiliated service: [**zlive.st**](https://zlive.st).

## What is zlive.st?

[zlive.st](https://zlive.st) is a free live TV and sports streaming platform affiliated with Z-Stream. It operates as a separate site focused entirely on live content, complementing Z-Stream's on-demand catalog. Like Z-Stream, it requires no signup and no subscription — just click and watch.

## What's Available on zlive.st

zlive.st carries a broad range of live channels and events, including:

- **World Cup** — live football coverage during major international tournaments
- **UFC** — live fight nights and pay-per-view events
- **Sports channels** — a wide selection of live sports from around the world
- **Live TV channels** — news, entertainment, and general broadcast channels streaming in real time

Coverage and available channels update around live events, so check the site directly to see what's currently streaming.

## How to Access zlive.st

You can reach zlive.st in two ways:

1. **Direct link** — go straight to [https://zlive.st](https://zlive.st) in your browser at any time.
2. **From Z-Stream** — look for the **zlive.st banner** on the Z-Stream homepage and click it. The link opens zlive.st in a separate tab so you don't lose your place on Z-Stream.

<Note>
  zlive.st is an affiliated service — it is a separate website from Z-Stream and opens in a new tab. Live content is only available on zlive.st and is not part of Z-Stream's on-demand library.
</Note>

## Live TV vs. On-Demand

It's worth understanding the distinction between the two services:

|  | Z-Stream | zlive.st |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content type** | On-demand movies and TV shows | Live TV channels and sports events |
| **URL** | [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov) | [zlive.st](https://zlive.st) |
| **Account required** | No | No |
| **Cost** | Free | Free |

Use Z-Stream when you want to pick something to watch on your own schedule. Switch to zlive.st when there's a live event you don't want to miss.

<Tip>
  Bookmark [https://zlive.st](https://zlive.st) separately in your browser for quick access during live events. When a match or fight is about to start, you'll be able to get there in one click without having to navigate through Z-Stream first.
</Tip>
