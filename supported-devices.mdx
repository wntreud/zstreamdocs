## No App Needed

Z-Stream runs entirely inside your web browser. There is nothing to download, nothing to install, and no operating system restrictions. As long as your device has a modern browser and an internet connection, you can watch.

Prefer an app? Z-Stream apps are available on Android, Android TV, Windows, and iOS for a faster, more seamless streaming experience.

## Supported Devices

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop & Laptop">
    Z-Stream works on all major desktop and laptop platforms with no configuration required.

    | Device | Notes | App Info |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Windows PC** | Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and most Chromium-based browsers. Windows 10 and 11 recommended. |  |
    | **Mac** | Works in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. macOS Ventura or later recommended for best Safari support. |  |

      On desktop, using a **wired (Ethernet) connection** instead of Wi-Fi gives you the most stable stream, especially for HD and 4K content. If you're on Wi-Fi, sit close to your router and make sure no other devices are saturating the connection.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile & Tablet">
    Z-Stream is fully usable on mobile and tablet devices through the browser — no app installation required.

    | Device | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Android Phone / Tablet** | Works in Chrome and Firefox for Android. Use the latest available version for the smoothest experience. |
    | **iPhone** | Works in Safari and Chrome on iOS 15 or later. Safari is the recommended browser on iPhone. |
    | **iPad** | Works in Safari and Chrome on iPadOS 15 or later. The larger screen makes for a great viewing experience. |

    <Tip>
      On mobile, connect to **Wi-Fi** rather than cellular data when streaming long content. Streaming over 4G/5G will work, but it consumes data quickly — especially at higher quality settings. Lower your screen brightness slightly to extend battery life during longer viewing sessions.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Smart TV">
    You can use Z-Stream on a smart TV by opening its built-in browser and navigating to [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov).

    | Device | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Samsung Smart TV** | Use the built-in Samsung Internet browser. Available on most Tizen-based models (2017 and newer). |
    | **LG Smart TV** | Use the built-in LG browser. Available on most webOS-based models (webOS 3.0 and newer). |
    | **Other Smart TVs** | Any smart TV with a built-in web browser should work, provided the browser is reasonably up to date and JavaScript is enabled. |

    <Tip>
      For the best smart TV experience, connect your TV to your router via **Ethernet** rather than Wi-Fi. This reduces buffering and provides a more consistent HD stream. If your TV's built-in browser feels slow, try casting from a nearby phone or laptop using your browser's cast feature.
    </Tip>

## Recommended Browsers

Z-Stream works with all major modern browsers. For the best experience, keep your browser updated to its latest version — browser updates frequently include performance and video playback improvements.

| Browser | Minimum Recommended Version | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Chrome** | Version 100 or later | Best overall compatibility. Recommended for most users. |
| **Mozilla Firefox** | Version 100 or later | Excellent performance and strong privacy defaults. |
| **Microsoft Edge** | Version 100 or later | Chromium-based; works identically to Chrome on Windows. |
| **Apple Safari** | Version 15 or later | Required on iPhone/iPad; works well on Mac too. |
| **Brave / Vivaldi / Opera** | Latest version | If you have a problem with opera switch browsers. |

<Note>
  Older browsers — such as Internet Explorer, or any browser that hasn't been updated in several years — are not supported. If you experience playback issues, the first step is always to update your browser to its latest version.
</Note>

## JavaScript Requirement

<Warning>
  **JavaScript must be enabled in your browser.** Z-Stream relies on JavaScript to load the player, fetch content metadata, and handle playback. If JavaScript is disabled, the site will not work.

  To re-enable JavaScript: open your browser's settings, navigate to **Privacy & Security** (or **Site Settings**), find the **JavaScript** option, and make sure it is set to **Allowed**.
</Warning>

## Tips for the Best Experience

<Tip>
  **Keep your browser up to date.** Browser vendors regularly release updates that improve video decoding, streaming performance, and security. An outdated browser is the most common cause of playback issues on Z-Stream.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Use a wired Ethernet connection for HD streaming.** A stable, low-latency connection makes a noticeable difference when watching in higher quality. If Wi-Fi is your only option, make sure you have a strong signal and minimal interference from other devices.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Close unused tabs and background apps.** Video playback is resource-intensive. Closing unneeded browser tabs and background applications frees up CPU and memory, which reduces buffering and improves overall smoothness — especially on older hardware.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Disable browser extensions if you have issues.** Some extensions (ad blockers, VPNs, script blockers, privacy tools) can interfere with video playback or site functionality. If Z-Stream isn't loading or playing correctly, try opening it in a private/incognito window — which disables most extensions by default — to see if an extension is the cause.
</Tip>
