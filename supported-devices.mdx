## No App Needed

Z-Stream runs entirely inside your web browser. There is nothing to download, nothing to install, and no operating system restrictions. As long as your device has a modern browser and an internet connection, you can watch.

Prefer an app? [Z-Stream apps](/apps) are available on Android, Android TV, Windows, and iOS for a faster, more seamless streaming experience.

## Supported Devices

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop & Laptop">
    Z-Stream works on all major desktop and laptop platforms with no configuration required.

    | Device | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Windows PC** | Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and most Chromium-based browsers. Windows 10 and 11 recommended. |
    | **Mac** | Works in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. macOS Ventura or later recommended for best Safari support. |
    | **Linux Desktop** | Works in Chrome and Firefox on all major distributions (Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian, Arch, etc.). |
    | **Chromebook** | Works natively in Chrome. ChromeOS devices are fully supported without any extra setup. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile & Tablet">
    Z-Stream is fully usable on mobile and tablet devices through the browser.

    | Device | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Android Phone / Tablet** | Works in Chrome and Firefox for Android. Use the latest available version for the smoothest experience. |
    | **iPhone** | Works in Safari and Chrome on iOS 15 or later. Safari is the recommended browser on iPhone. |
    | **iPad** | Works in Safari and Chrome on iPadOS 15 or later. The larger screen makes for a great viewing experience. |

    <Tip>
      On mobile, connect to **Wi-Fi** rather than cellular data when streaming long content. Streaming over 4G/5G will work, but it consumes data quickly — especially at higher quality settings. Lower your screen brightness slightly to extend battery life during longer viewing sessions.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Recommended Browsers

Z-Stream works with all major modern browsers. For the best experience, keep your browser updated to its latest version — browser updates frequently include performance and video playback improvements.

| Browser | Minimum Recommended Version | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Chrome** | Version 100 or later | Best overall compatibility. Recommended for most users. |
| **Mozilla Firefox** | Version 100 or later | Excellent performance and strong privacy defaults. |
| **Microsoft Edge** | Version 100 or later | Chromium-based; works identically to Chrome on Windows. |
| **Apple Safari** | Version 15 or later | Required on iPhone/iPad; works well on Mac too. |
| **Brave / Vivaldi / Opera** | Latest version | If you have a problem with opera switch browsers. |

<Note>
  Older browsers — such as Internet Explorer, or any browser that hasn't been updated in several years — are not supported. If you experience playback issues, the first step is always to update your browser to its latest version.
</Note>

## JavaScript Requirement

<Warning>
  **JavaScript must be enabled in your browser.** Z-Stream relies on JavaScript to load the player, fetch content metadata, and handle playback. If JavaScript is disabled, the site will not work.

  To re-enable JavaScript: open your browser's settings, navigate to **Privacy & Security** (or **Site Settings**), find the **JavaScript** option, and make sure it is set to **Allowed**.
</Warning>
