Z-Stream lets you watch thousands of movies and TV shows instantly from any browser — no account, no download, and no payment required. This page walks you through finding a title and starting playback in just a few clicks.

## Start Watching in Four Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Z-Stream in your browser">
    Navigate to [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov) in any modern browser on your phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop. No installation or sign-up is needed — the full library is available the moment the page loads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search for content">
    You have two ways to find something to watch:

    - **Search** — Type a title, actor, or keyword into the search bar at the top of the page to get instant results.
    - **Discover** — Head to the [Discover page](https://zstream.mov/discover) to explore the full library using genre filters, popularity charts, editorial picks, and more. See [Finding Content on the Discover Page](#finding-content-on-the-discover-page) below for a full breakdown of every section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a title to open its detail page">
    Select any movie or TV show from the results to open its detail page. Here you'll find the synopsis, cast, release year, runtime, rating, and — for TV shows — a full episode list organized by season.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Play to start streaming">
    Hit the **Play** button to begin playback immediately in your browser. No buffering screens, no ad breaks, and no prompts asking you to log in or subscribe — just the content, right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding Content on the Discover Page

The [Discover page](https://zstream.mov/discover) is your home base for exploring the Z-Stream library. It organizes content into several sections so you can always find something worth watching, whether you know exactly what you want or are just browsing.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Genre Browsing" icon="grid-2">
    Filter the entire library by genre. Available genres include Action, Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Crime, Documentary, Drama, Fantasy, Horror, Mystery, Romance, Science Fiction, Thriller, and more. Select any genre to see a curated grid of matching titles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Most Popular" icon="fire">
    See what the Z-Stream community is watching right now. The Most Popular section is updated regularly and is split into separate lists for **Movies** and **TV Shows**, so you can track trending content in each category independently.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Top Rated" icon="star">
    Browse titles ranked by audience score. Top Rated surfaces the highest-quality content in the library regardless of release date — a great starting point if you want something reliably good.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="In Cinemas / Now Playing" icon="film">
    Catch up on the latest theatrical releases. The In Cinemas (Now Playing) section highlights movies that are currently showing in theaters, so you can watch alongside the cultural conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Editor Picks" icon="bookmark">
    Hand-curated recommendations selected by the Z-Stream editorial team. Editor Picks highlight hidden gems, acclaimed classics, and standout new additions that might not surface in algorithmic charts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Switching Between Movies and TV Shows

At the top of the browse and Discover pages you'll find two tabs: **Movies** and **TV Shows**. Clicking either tab filters every section on the page — genre results, Most Popular, Top Rated, and more — to show only that content type. Switch between them freely at any time to narrow your focus without losing your place in the page.
