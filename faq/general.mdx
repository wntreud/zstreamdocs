Z-Stream is a free, no-account streaming platform for movies and TV shows. Whether you're new to the service or just have a quick question, the answers below cover everything you need to get started and make the most of what Z-Stream offers.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Z-Stream really free?">
    Yes, Z-Stream is completely free to use — no subscription, no trial period, and no hidden charges. You can browse and watch any movie or TV show on the platform without ever entering a payment method. There are no paywalls or premium tiers; every title is available to everyone at no cost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to create an account?">
    No, you do not need to create an account or register in any way. Simply visit [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov), find something you want to watch, and press play — no sign-up form, no email address, and no password required. Z-Stream is designed to get you watching as quickly as possible with zero friction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Z-Stream legal to use?">
    Z-Stream operates in a legal grey area that varies by country and jurisdiction. We recommend reviewing the copyright and streaming laws applicable in your region before using the service. If you have concerns, using a VPN and consulting local regulations is advisable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Z-Stream the same as P-Stream?">
    Yes — Z-Stream is the direct successor to P-Stream, which was previously available at pstream.net. The platform has been rebranded and relaunched at [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov) with the same mission: free, no-account streaming for everyone. If you were a P-Stream user, you'll find Z-Stream immediately familiar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to install an app?">
    No installation is required. Z-Stream runs entirely in your web browser, so you can access it on any device that has a modern browser — desktop, laptop, tablet, or smartphone. Just navigate to [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov) and start watching instantly. Native apps for Android, Android TV, iOS, and Windows are also available on the [apps page](/apps) for a smoother experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Z-Stream available in my country?">
    Z-Stream is accessible from most countries around the world via any standard web browser. However, some ISPs or regional networks may block access to the site, in which case a VPN can help you connect reliably. If you're having trouble reaching the site, check the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/streaming) for the latest mirror links or connection tips.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a problem or give feedback?">
    The best place to report issues or share feedback is the official [Z-Stream Discord server](https://discord.gg/streaming). The community and support team are active there and can help troubleshoot playback problems, broken links, or missing content. Providing specific details — like the title you were trying to watch and your browser/device — helps the team respond faster.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?">
    Yes — Z-Stream has native apps for **Android**, **Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV**, **iOS**, and **Windows**. Download them from [zstream.mov/apps](https://zstream.mov/apps), and see the [apps page](/apps) for full install instructions. The website also works great in mobile browsers if you'd rather not install anything.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I save and sync my watch history between devices?">
    Click the menu button in the top-right corner of the site, then click **Sync to cloud** and make an account. You'll be able to log in with this account on every device, and your **settings and watch history** will stay in sync.

    Z-Stream will not ask you for an email or password. You log in through either a **Passkey** or a **12-word random passphrase**. Save your passphrase somewhere safe — it's the only way to recover your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream have a community?">
    Yes! Z-Stream has an active and welcoming community on Discord. You can join at [discord.gg/streaming](https://discord.gg/streaming) to chat with other users, get help with technical issues, stay updated on new features, and share content recommendations. It's also the fastest way to reach the team directly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
