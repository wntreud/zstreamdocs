Your privacy matters, and Z-Stream is built from the ground up to respect it. Because the platform requires no account and no payment, very little information is ever involved. The answers below explain exactly what Z-Stream does and doesn't do with your data.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream collect my personal data?">
    Z-Stream does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information, so there is no profile data, name, or contact information collected from you. Because there's no login system, your identity is never tied to your activity on the platform. Your use of the site is essentially anonymous by default.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to give my email or payment details?">
    No — Z-Stream never asks for your email address, credit card, bank details, or any other personal or financial information. The platform is entirely free and account-free, so there is simply no reason for you to hand over any sensitive data. If you ever encounter a page claiming to require payment or registration to use Z-Stream, it is not the official site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream use cookies or tracking?">
    Z-Stream may use minimal, functional browser storage (such as cookies or local storage) to remember preferences like your chosen language or player settings within a session. Z-Stream does display ads to keep the service free, but does not deploy behavioral profiling tools or third-party analytics that follow you across the web. Your browsing activity on Z-Stream is not used to build a personal profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my viewing history stored?">
    Z-Stream does not maintain a server-side record of what you watch. Since there are no user accounts, your viewing activity is never linked to any personal identity on the platform. Any watch history that appears within the site (such as recently viewed titles) is stored locally in your own browser and is never shared with Z-Stream's servers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream show ads?">
    Yes. Z-Stream is free to use, and running ads is how the platform stays online and covers infrastructure costs. You may see ads while browsing or before/around playback. Z-Stream does its best to keep ads non-intrusive and free of malware, but occasional pop-ups or redirects from third-party ad networks can happen. Using an ad blocker such as **uBlock Origin** is fully supported, though note that some sources or scrapers may fail if your blocker is too aggressive — see the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Z-Stream safe to use?">
    Z-Stream is designed to be a safe environment on the official site at [zstream.mov](https://zstream.mov). Ads are served through vetted networks and the site actively works to keep malware, misleading pop-ups, and intrusive redirects out. As with any website, it's good practice to keep your browser and operating system up to date. Be cautious of copycat sites that may impersonate Z-Stream — always verify you're on the correct domain, and check the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) for any official mirror or alternate links.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream share data with third parties?">
    Because Z-Stream does not collect personal data or require accounts, there is no personal information to share with third parties. The platform does not sell, rent, or otherwise pass user data to advertisers, data brokers, or any other outside parties. Your visit to Z-Stream remains between you and the site.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
