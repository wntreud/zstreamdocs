Z-Stream hosts a wide and growing library of movies and TV shows, all free and available without an account. If you're wondering what's available, how to find a specific title, or where to watch live content, the answers below will point you in the right direction.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many movies and TV shows are on Z-Stream?">
    Z-Stream hosts an extensive and growing library covering thousands of movies and TV shows across a wide range of genres, languages, and decades. The catalog spans everything from classic films and cult favorites to recent blockbusters and ongoing series. You can browse by genre or search by title to discover what's available at any time, but
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are new movies and shows added regularly?">
    Yes, the Z-Stream library is updated on an ongoing basis as new content becomes available. Recent theatrical releases, newly aired TV episodes, and newly available titles are added as streams become accessible. For the latest additions and announcements, keep an eye on the [Z-Stream Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) where updates are shared with the community.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I find a specific title?">
    If a title doesn't appear when you search or browse, it may not yet be available in the Z-Stream library, or it could be temporarily unlisted while a working stream is sourced. Try searching by the full title or an alternate spelling to rule out a mismatch. If you're confident the title should be there, you can request it or flag the gap in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/streaming).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream have the latest cinema releases?">
    Z-Stream often carries recent releases, but availability depends on when a watchable stream becomes accessible after a title's theatrical or digital release. Some new films appear shortly after hitting theaters, while others may take longer depending on how quickly streams are sourced. Check the site regularly or follow the Discord for notifications about newly added releases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request a movie or TV show?">
    Yes — the best way to request a specific title is to visit the [Z-Stream Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) and post in the appropriate requests channel. The team and community review requests regularly and will add titles when a reliable stream becomes available. While not every request can be fulfilled immediately, frequently requested titles are prioritized.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there subtitles or dubbed versions available?">
    Many titles on Z-Stream include subtitle options, and dubbed versions are available for some content. Subtitle and dub availability varies by title — check the player controls when watching to see which options are offered. If a subtitle track or dubbed version you need is missing, you're welcome to request it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was a title removed?">
    Titles are occasionally removed when their stream sources go down, become unavailable, or are taken offline. In some cases, content may be temporarily unavailable while a replacement source is found. If you notice a title has disappeared that you'd like back, report it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) so the team can look into restoring it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Z-Stream have anime?">
    Yes, Z-Stream includes anime titles alongside its movie and TV show catalog. You can search for specific series or films by name, or browse available genres to find anime content. As with all content, availability varies and the library continues to grow — request specific series in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) if you can't find what you're looking for.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I watch live sports and TV?">
    Z-Stream focuses on on-demand movies and TV shows. For live TV and sports streaming, check out the affiliated live TV service at [zlive.st](https://zlive.st), which is designed specifically for live content. You can also ask in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/streaming) for the latest information on live coverage and channel availability.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
