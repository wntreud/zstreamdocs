<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Why is the video buffering or slow?">
    Buffering is most often caused by your internet connection speed or network congestion — try pausing the video for 30–60 seconds to let it pre-load, or lowering the video quality in the player settings. Switching to a wired connection or moving closer to your Wi-Fi router can also make a significant difference. If the problem persists across multiple titles, the specific stream source may be under heavy load; try refreshing the page or selecting an alternative source if one is available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change video quality?">
    Yes, most titles on Z-Stream allow you to adjust the video quality directly within the player. Look for the settings or gear icon in the player controls to choose from available resolutions such as 480p, 720p, or 1080p. Selecting a lower quality can help reduce buffering on slower connections, while higher quality delivers a sharper picture on faster networks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are subtitles available?">
    Subtitles are available for many titles on Z-Stream and can be toggled on or off using the subtitle/CC button in the player. Multiple language options may be available depending on the title — check the subtitle menu within the player to see what's offered. If subtitles are missing for a title you're watching, you can request them or report the gap in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/wmbWfk4SGy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The video won't play — what do I do?">
    First, try refreshing the page and clicking play again, as temporary source errors often resolve on their own. If the video still won't start, try a different browser (Chrome or Firefox are recommended) or clear your browser's cache and cookies. For persistent issues, report the broken title in the [Z-Stream Discord](https://discord.gg/wmbWfk4SGy) with the title name and your browser details so the team can investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I watch in full screen?">
    Yes — you can enter full-screen mode by clicking the full-screen icon in the bottom-right corner of the video player, or by pressing the **F** key on your keyboard. To exit full screen, press **Escape** or click the icon again. On mobile, rotating your device to landscape orientation will also expand the player to fill the screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the audio out of sync?">
    Audio sync issues can sometimes occur due to browser performance or the specific stream source being used. Try pausing the video, waiting a few seconds, and resuming playback — this often resets the buffer and corrects the sync. If the problem continues, refreshing the page or trying a different browser may resolve it; you can also report persistent sync issues in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/wmbWfk4SGy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use keyboard shortcuts to control playback?">
    Yes, Z-Stream's player supports several handy keyboard shortcuts. Press **Space** or **K** to play/pause, **F** for full screen, **M** to mute, and the **Left/Right arrow keys** to seek backward or forward. Using keyboard shortcuts makes it easy to control playback without reaching for your mouse, especially in full-screen mode.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does the player say 'an error occurred'?">
    This error typically means the stream source for that title is temporarily unavailable or has expired. Try refreshing the page first, and if the error persists, check whether an alternative source can be selected within the player. If no sources work, the issue is likely on the server side — reporting it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/wmbWfk4SGy) helps the team restore the stream as quickly as possible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cast to my TV (Chromecast, AirPlay)?">
    Casting support depends on your browser and device. Google Chrome supports Chromecast — click the Cast button in your browser's toolbar or use the browser menu to send the tab to your TV. AirPlay is available on Safari on macOS and iOS devices; look for the AirPlay icon in the player or in your system controls. For the best casting experience, make sure your browser and streaming device are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
