Having trouble with playback, or just want to get the most out of Z-Stream's video player? The questions below address the most common playback scenarios, from buffering and quality settings to subtitles and casting.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I switch sources and get more sources?">
    While the video is playing, tap the settings/sources icon in the player, then choose another entry under **Video Sources**. Trying a different source is the #1 fix for playback errors, wrong episodes, and audio/video issues.<br /><br />To get more sources try enabling the browser extension: [https://zstream.mov/onboarding](https://zstream.mov/onboarding)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the video buffering or slow?">
    Buffering is most often caused by your internet connection speed or network congestion — try pausing the video for 30–60 seconds to let it pre-load, or lowering the video quality in the player settings. Switching to a wired connection or moving closer to your Wi-Fi router can also make a significant difference. If the problem persists across multiple titles, the specific stream source may be under heavy load; try refreshing the page or selecting an alternative source if one is available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change video quality?">
    Many titles on Z-Stream allow you to adjust video quality directly within the player — look for a settings or gear icon in the player controls to choose from available resolutions. Selecting a lower quality can help reduce buffering on slower connections, while higher quality delivers a sharper picture on faster networks. Quality options vary by title and the available stream source.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are subtitles available?">
    Subtitles are available for many titles on Z-Stream and can be toggled using the subtitle or CC option in the player controls. Availability varies by title — check the subtitle menu within the player to see which languages are offered for what you're watching. If subtitles are missing for a title you need, you can report the gap in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/streaming).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The video won't play — what do I do?">
    First, try refreshing the page and clicking play again, as temporary source errors often resolve on their own. If the video still won't start, try a different browser (Chrome or Firefox are recommended) or clear your browser's cache and cookies. For persistent issues, report the broken title in the [Z-Stream Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) with the title name and your browser details so the team can investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I watch in full screen?">
    Yes — you can enter full-screen mode by clicking the full-screen icon in the player controls. On most keyboards you can also press **F** to toggle full screen, and **Escape** to exit. On mobile, rotating your device to landscape orientation will also expand the player to fill the screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is the audio out of sync?">
    Audio sync issues can sometimes occur due to browser performance or the specific stream source being used. Try pausing the video, waiting a few seconds, and resuming playback — this often resets the buffer and corrects the sync. If the problem continues, refreshing the page or trying a different browser may resolve it; you can also report persistent sync issues in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use keyboard shortcuts to control playback?">
    Z-Stream's player supports keyboard shortcuts for common controls. You can typically press **Space** to play or pause, **F** for full screen, **M** to mute, and the **Left/Right arrow keys** to seek backward or forward. Exact shortcut availability may vary depending on the player version in use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does the player say 'an error occurred'?">
    This error typically means the stream source for that title is temporarily unavailable or has expired. Try refreshing the page first, and if the error persists, check whether an alternative source can be selected within the player. If no sources work, the issue is likely on the server side — reporting it in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) helps the team restore the stream as quickly as possible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cast to my TV (Chromecast, AirPlay)?">
    Casting support depends on your browser and device rather than Z-Stream itself. Google Chrome supports Chromecast — use your browser's cast option to send the tab to your TV. AirPlay is available on Safari on macOS and iOS; look for the AirPlay icon in your system controls. For the best experience, make sure your browser and streaming device are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Red / green weird color filter on videos">
    This commonly happens on 4K with HDR or Dolby Vision content when your device doesn't support HDR properly or has poor calibration. To fix it:

    1. Reduce the quality to **1080p** in the player settings.
    2. Try switching to a different source in the player.
    3. Switch to **Google Chrome**, which tends to handle HDR fallback best.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Page not loading">
    Which browser are you using?

    - **Opera / Opera GX** — these browsers frequently break Z-Stream. Switch to a different browser.
    - **Chrome** — try updating Chrome to the latest version. If it still doesn't load, switch browsers. **Firefox** is a great option.
    - **Safari / Edge / Brave** — make sure the browser is on its latest version and that JavaScript is enabled.

    If the page still won't load after switching browsers, see the [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Failed to scrape / failed to play video">
    Try these hotfixes in order:

    1. **Turn on a VPN.** Your ISP, school, or network may be blocking the source.
    2. **Turn off your VPN** if you already have one enabled — some VPN IPs are blocked by sources.
    3. **Turn off your ad blocker** on Z-Stream. Aggressive blockers can prevent scrapers from reaching sources.
    4. **Switch to a different source** in the player.
    5. **If you're using the userscript**, make sure you're on the latest version.

    Most commonly, your ISP, school, or internet provider has blocked the sources in question. If nothing works, report it in the [Z-Stream Discord](https://discord.gg/streaming) with:

    - Your error / debug log
    - The media title (and episode \+ season if it's a TV show)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why am I seeing a black screen?">
    A black screen with audio (or no video at all) is usually caused by a corrupt or broken video file on that specific source. Switch to a different source in the player to fix it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The episode I'm watching is incorrect / wrong episode plays">
    Switch to a different source in the player. Episode and season metadata is provided by third-party sources, and Z-Stream does not control content accuracy on individual sources.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
